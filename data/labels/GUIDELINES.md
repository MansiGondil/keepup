# Topic labeling guidelines
Tag each article with the topics it is MAINLY about (1 to 3 tags,
comma-separated). Leave the cell empty if no topic fits. Judge from the
title and snippet only; do not open the article. When unsure, leave the
tag out.
- ai: AI products, models, companies, AI policy and regulation, AI research
- data-ml: data tools and engineering, analytics, ML techniques, datasets,
  benchmarks, ML papers
- sre: reliability and operations: outages and incidents, postmortems,
  observability, on-call, Kubernetes, cloud and infrastructure
- security: vulnerabilities, breaches, cyber-attacks, hacking, privacy
  incidents
- business: companies, markets, the economy, deals
- world: international and political news, public affairs
- health, money (personal finance, prices, taxes, cost of living),
  science (research, space, environment), careers (jobs, workplace,
  hiring), sports, entertainment
Notes: an arXiv paper on a new ML method is data-ml (add ai if it is about
LLMs or agents). A story can have several tags, for example a cloud outage
is sre and may also be business.
